Two additions for producers and pools. No consensus change: this is
v1.3-DIMEBAG's chain, with Midstate Walk from block **37,300** and midstate's
from **371,500**. Nodes that only follow the chain need not upgrade. A pool
that wants to take co-bonds, or a miner with an NVIDIA GPU, does.

## Co-bonds while the node runs

A pool's midwimble blocks count in full only while enough bonds stand behind
its hashrate, so its capacity grows with the co-bonds locked on its mining
key. Until now a co-bond reached a node only as a file loaded at startup, and
its registration proof aged out within a day or two.

A node started with `--mining-bond` now takes co-bonds while it runs, from no
more than the coin:

- `GET /co-bonds` returns the node's mining key, its own bond's id, and its
  co-bonds with their progress.
- `POST /co-bonds` takes `{script, value, salt}` and hands one in.

The node takes only a bond on its own key whose coin is on midstate. The bond
must hold what a new one must, be locked long enough to count, and be within
the one-year horizon. The node then registers it itself, through its
`--midstate-rpc`, as `midwimble bond register` would:

- it takes a proof of the coin;
- it waits for a day of work above the proof;
- its next blocks carry the registration;
- it takes a fresh proof whenever the relay is about to pass the old one.

It signs with each co-bond once it is ready, and keeps them in `co-bonds.json`
in its data directory. `--co-bond` files work as before.

The RPC stays loopback-only. `midstate pool` (v6.4.0) publishes the key and
takes hand-ins at `/pool/api/co-bonds`, and can pay co-bonders. Midstate's
desktop wallet co-bonds with a pool by its address. See `docs/BONDED_POW.md`
and `docs/POOL_MERGED_MINING.md`.

## Midstate Walk on NVIDIA GPUs

In a `gpu` build (the release binaries), the node's miner, `pool-mine` and
`merge-mine` mine Midstate Walk on CUDA GPUs, beside the CPU or instead of it:

- `--backend auto|gpu|cpu`;
- `MINER_CUDA_DEVICES` picks GPUs, and `MINER_CUDA_WALKS` sizes their batches;
- `gpu-info` lists the GPUs and checks each against the known answers.

The NVIDIA driver is loaded when mining starts, so a machine without one mines
on its CPU as before. Every hash a GPU reports is checked on the CPU before it
becomes a share or a block.

## Upgrading

- **Pools that merge-mine and want co-bonds:** run this release on the
  midwimble node, with `--mining-bond` and `--midstate-rpc`, behind
  `midstate pool` v6.4.0. Keep the node's RPC on loopback.
- **Everyone else:** optional. v1.3-DIMEBAG follows the same chain.
